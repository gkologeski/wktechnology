# Contratos

- Imported PDF bodies are read server-side (`pdf-body.server.ts`, unpdf) and every imported body passes `stripControlChars` before insert; why: browser pdf.js failed silently and NUL chars from PDFs make the database reject the whole body.
- Pending legacy discard is append-only and uses the caller's contract view/update/delete plus legacy-view access; why: originals and history must survive, while operationally linked or regularized contracts must not be discarded.
- Purchase contracts for a sale are created by `purchase-from-sale.server.ts` (user RLS, PJ or PF counterparty, internal traceability through parent/contract_purchase_sales only); they never inherit the customer's quote, proposal, deal or title. Purchase technical proposals use the separate native catalogue and immutable revisions in `purchase-proposal.*`; why: the supplier must receive only its own scope, prices and dates.
- Contract attachments declare their audience before new external sharing. Explicitly reviewed internal files remain in the record and stay out of signature packages; unknown uploads block new sharing. Live economic-content permission and audience gates protect private downloads, cached packages, native/ForSign dispatch and public capabilities; why: customer and supplier documents must remain separate while historical bytes and already issued operations survive.
- Purchase payables are proposed by pure `purchase-payables.ts` and only created by Finance through the `contract_purchase_payables_generate` RPC after signature; why: no automatic finance entries and one idempotent, sum-checked path.
- Purchase contract term is suggested by pure `purchase-term.ts` (start not in the past; end = latest of sale, active amendments, linked project due date); why: the supplier must cover the delivery period without retyping.
- A purchase contract may serve several sales: primary = parent_contract_id, extra sales only via `contract_purchase_sale_set` into `contract_purchase_sales`; why: one supplier contract can cover a sale plus amendments with their own deals without moving the nesting.
- Main contracts show consolidated terms from active amendments via pure `effective-terms.ts` (end date, monthly value, hours/month, notice and scope replaced when different, even shorter; value and hour packages summed; amendment start never replaces the contract start); why: one rule for the contract page and next-step deadline.
- Contract signature modals prefill CONTRATANTE/CONTRATADO representatives and display each party's legal name via `suggestContractSigners` (caller RLS + `hiring_contract_signature_context`, respecting `techcontracts.signature_evidence.manage.workspace` and `techsales.contacts.view.*`); why: e-signature always signs by individual legal representative while keeping company identity visible without manual lookup.
- Public contract draft links (`/minuta/$token`) load the same-workspace locked linked proposal server-side via `contract-draft-public.server.ts`, strip internal IDs and `public_token` before returning to the browser, and serve its PDF only through `/api/public/contract-drafts/$token/proposal-pdf`; why: revoking or reopening the draft revokes both the draft and proposal PDF together without leaking proposal tokens.
- E-sign body headers separate TechContracts purchase contracts (`hiring_contract_esign_body_without_attachments_purchase_v2`: Contratante = WK, Contratada / Fornecedor = PJ legal_name+CNPJ or PF contact, values/payment summary, no empty Services footer) from sales (`_sales_v1`) and TechHire (`hiring_request_origin`), keeping sales/TechHire fingerprints byte-identical; why: purchase PDFs never invert party roles and stale inverted packages are never reused.
