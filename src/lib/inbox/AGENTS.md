# Inbox

- Histórico de conversas da Inbox sai de `listConversationMessages` (cursor `(created_at,id)`, página recente primeiro) via `useMessageHistory` + `MessageHistoryViewport`; tempo real sempre filtrado pela conversa e trata recusa vinda na mensagem "system"; por quê: conversas longas sem corte, sem baixar corpos de e-mail e sem canal global.
- Chat ao vivo: servidor exige `techsales.inbox.chat.<ação>` e a RLS limita sessões/mensagens ao escopo via `my_perm_workspaces`; por quê: sem leitura ou envio em conversas de terceiros.
- Canonical human chat uses authenticated INSERT with inbox_chat_request; its AFTER INSERT trigger records acceptance atomically, never admin INSERT or UPDATE; why: the full real row-policy set must authorize transport and retries cannot promote mirrors.
- Chat INSERT retains the published 0122 own/workspace predicate; team-only is not a sending scope. Inbox resumes copy the root's private occurrence time and validate their deterministic resume key and current parent chain; why: preserve existing access and reject forged, revoked or pre-cutoff resumptions.
- Gmail transport acceptance is append-only before thread reads; projection links back to that immutable acceptance and keeps the canonical provider uniqueness. Explicit Gmail/Unipile attempts compare the full send payload; why: retries must reconcile without resending and changed recipients/files cannot reuse an accepted operation.
- Human Gmail acceptances drain through the authorized sync before provider discovery, using a private bounded claim queue and atomic service-only projection with SQLSTATE audit/backoff; History API skips accepted provider IDs. Why: closed composers and stale history cannot lose or promote an acceptance, expose its payload, or resend it.
